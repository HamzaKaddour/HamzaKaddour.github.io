Multi-Temporal SAR River-Ice Segmentation

Research pipeline for river-ice classification using Sentinel-1 SAR, weak optical supervision, temporal context, transformer backbones, temperature information, explainability, and transfer analysis toward NISAR.

SensorSentinel-1 SAR
SupervisionSentinel-2 SCL
ModelsMaxViT + Swin
TransferSentinel-1 → NISAR
Research & publications

Problem

River-ice monitoring is difficult because reliable labels are sparse, optical observations are cloud-limited, and SAR signatures vary with surface condition, geometry, and season. The project investigates how weak optical labels and temporal SAR context can support scalable segmentation.

Input design

The model uses Sentinel-1 VV, VH, polarization ratio/difference features, temporal observations, and ERA5-Land 2 m temperature context. Sentinel-2 Scene Classification Layer observations are used as weak supervision for water and snow/ice classes.

Research extensions

  • Independent validation and label-quality analysis
  • Cross-AOI / cross-year generalization
  • Temperature ablation
  • Interpretability through occlusion, GradientSHAP, and Grad-CAM
  • Sentinel-1-to-NISAR transfer with polarization-aware preprocessing
  • VIIRS/SAR harmonization and downscaling exploration

Engineering depth

The work includes geospatial preprocessing, large raster handling, temporal alignment, HDF5-to-raster conversion for NISAR, GPU-aware inference, and reproducible evaluation in addition to model development.
